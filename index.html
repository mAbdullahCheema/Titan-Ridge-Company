Who we are

A single, dependable source for construction, supply and project support

Titan Ridge Company provides reliable, safe and cost-effective solutions to the construction, infrastructure, industrial and renewable-energy sectors in the Kingdom. Our management brings extensive industry experience developed on major projects — with leading contractors, solar-energy companies, suppliers and industrial service providers.

That practical experience gives us a working understanding of project requirements, material procurement, construction support, workforce management, quality expectations and the importance of timely delivery.

Our business model is built around giving clients a single, dependable source for construction, supply, manpower and project support. Whether we are supporting a major EPC contractor, a renewable-energy project, an industrial facility, an infrastructure development or a smaller construction requirement, we tailor the solution to the project's technical, commercial and schedule requirements.

We aim to establish long-term relationships with clients, contractors, suppliers and business partners through professionalism, responsiveness, quality, safety, integrity and dependable delivery.
